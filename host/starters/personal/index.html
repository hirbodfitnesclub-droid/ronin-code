About

م

Good design, to me, is mostly editing — removing everything that competes with the one thing a person came to do.

I started in graphic design in Tehran, moved into product work when I realised the most interesting layout problem is a flow, not a page. Since then I've led design for banking apps used by millions, a telehealth service for rural clinics, and tools for teachers who have ten minutes, not ten hours.

Outside client work I mentor early-career designers, collect Persian typography specimens, and keep a small, stubborn garden.
